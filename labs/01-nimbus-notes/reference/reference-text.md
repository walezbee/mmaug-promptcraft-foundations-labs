Nimbus Notes — Help Center FAQ | Lab Version 1.0

Fictional training product. Nimbus Notes is a note-taking and light project-management app for individuals and small teams. This document is the sole authorized product reference for the bootcamp labs.

Plans & pricing

Q: What plans does Nimbus Notes offer?

A: Three plans: Free, Plus ($6 per user/month), and Team ($12 per user/month). All paid plans are billed monthly or annually, with a discount applied for annual billing.

Q: How much storage do I get on each plan?

A: Free includes 1 GB of storage for a single user. Plus includes 20 GB per user. Team includes 100 GB of pooled workspace storage plus 20 GB per individual user.

Q: Is there a free trial?

A: The Plus plan includes a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. The Free plan has no time limit and does not expire.

Using the app

Q: Can I use Nimbus Notes offline?

A: Plus and Team plans support full offline editing, with automatic sync once you're back online. The Free plan requires an internet connection.

Q: Which devices and platforms are supported?

A: Nimbus Notes is available as a web app (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and as native apps for Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android. There is no native Linux app; Linux users can use the web app.

Q: Can I export my notes?

A: Yes. Any note can be exported individually as PDF, Markdown, or plain text from the note's menu at any time. Bulk export of an entire workspace is available on the Team plan only.

Collaboration

Q: Can I share notes and collaborate with others?

A: Plus lets you share an individual note with up to 3 collaborators. Team allows unlimited collaborators within a shared workspace, with real-time co-editing.

Q: How many people can be in one Team workspace?

A: A single Team workspace supports up to 50 members. Larger workspaces can be created by contacting support to split members across multiple linked workspaces.

Security & billing

Q: How is my data secured?

A: Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and encrypted at rest using AES-256. Team workspaces additionally support role-based permissions (Owner, Editor, Viewer).

Q: What is your refund policy?

A: New subscribers to Plus or Team get a 14-day money-back guarantee on their first payment. Refunds are not issued after the 14-day window, and renewals are non-refundable.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

A: Go to Account Settings > Subscription and select Cancel. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period; there are no partial refunds for unused time outside the 14-day guarantee.

Support

Q: How do I contact support, and what are your hours?

A: Email support@nimbusnotes.example. Our team responds Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM GMT. Team plan subscribers are placed in a priority queue. We do not currently offer live chat or phone support.

Training scope and knowledge boundaries

Use this FAQ as the only source for product-specific answers in the exercises. Do not assume that undocumented capabilities exist or do not exist. If the FAQ does not answer a question, say that the information is not documented here; direct the user to the support contact stated above when appropriate.

Intentionally undocumented topics include third-party integrations (Microsoft Teams, Slack, Google Drive), AI meeting summaries, meeting transcription, API availability, and data residency. These omissions are deliberate evaluation cases, not statements that the features are unavailable.
