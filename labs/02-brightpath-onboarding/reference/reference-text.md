1. Welcome to Brightpath Logistics

Q: Overview

A: This fact sheet provides essential information to help new employees prepare for their first day, understand first-week activities, and locate support contacts. For the training exercise, it is the authoritative onboarding reference.

2. First Day — Arrival and Registration

Q: When and where should I report?

A: Report at 9:00 AM to the Riverside office, 220 Harbor Street, Suite 400. Ask for reception; they will direct you to the HR welcome session.

Q: What should I bring?

A: Bring a government-issued ID and a voided check or bank details for payroll setup.

Q: When will I receive my laptop and credentials?

A: Laptop and login credentials are distributed during the Day 1 HR session.

3. First-Week Onboarding Schedule

Q: What happens in the first week?

A: Day 1: Orientation and IT setup. Days 2–3: Role-specific training with your manager. Days 4–5: Shadowing a team member.

4. Workplace Expectations

Q: What is the dress code?

A: Business casual Monday–Thursday; casual on Fridays.

Q: Is parking available?

A: Free employee parking is available in the Harbor Street garage. Your badge is issued on Day 1.

5. Probation and Performance Check-ins

Q: How long is probation?

A: The first 90 days are a probationary period.

Q: When are manager check-ins?

A: At 30 and 90 days.

6. Employee Benefits

Q: When does health insurance start?

A: Coverage begins on the 1st of the month following 30 days of employment.

Q: When does 401(k) enrollment open?

A: Automatically after 90 days; HR will email instructions.

Q: How much vacation do full-time employees accrue?

A: 15 vacation days per year, starting from Day 1.

7. Payroll

Q: How often is payroll processed?

A: Bi-weekly, every other Friday.

Q: How are employees paid?

A: Via direct deposit.

8. IT and HR Support

Q: Who handles IT setup issues?

A: IT Helpdesk: it-help@brightpathlogistics.example or extension 204.

Q: Who handles benefits and policy questions?

A: HR Generalist Maria Chen: hr@brightpathlogistics.example or extension 201.

9. Knowledge Base Scope and Limitations

Covered: first-day arrival and documents; IT setup; first-week schedule; dress code and parking; probation and check-ins; health insurance, 401(k), vacation; payroll; HR and IT contacts.

Not documented: remote or hybrid work; sick leave; home internet or equipment reimbursement; meal, transport, or relocation allowances; overtime or holiday pay; detailed insurance premiums; salary amounts; additional leave and approval procedures.

These omissions are intentional for the lab. Missing information does not establish that a benefit or policy does not exist.
